Setup guide

How to get your Alpaca keys

Follow these steps in your Alpaca paper-trading dashboard.

  1. Open your paper account.
    Sign in to Alpaca and switch to the paper-trading environment.
  2. Click “Generate New Keys”.
    Find the API Keys section in your paper-trading dashboard.
  3. Copy both credentials.
    Copy the Key ID and Secret Key. When KokuTrade asks you to connect, use the one-time link it sends to WhatsApp, enter the verification code, and submit your keys on that secure page.
Important: Your Secret Key may only be displayed once. Copy and store it securely when generated. Never send your Secret Key as an ordinary WhatsApp message. KokuTrade's secure linking page validates and encrypts your credentials.
