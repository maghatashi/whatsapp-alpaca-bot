Setup guide

How to get your Alpaca keys

Follow these steps in your Alpaca paper-trading dashboard.

  1. Open your paper account.
    Sign in to Alpaca and switch to the paper-trading environment.
  2. Click “Generate New Keys”.
    Find the API Keys section in your paper-trading dashboard.
  3. Copy both credentials.
    Copy the Key ID and Secret Key to the secure connection flow provided by your KokuTrade bot.
Important: Your Secret Key may only be displayed once. Copy and store it securely when it is generated. Do not send it in an ordinary WhatsApp message unless your bot's connection flow is explicitly designed and secured for credential handling.
